Head Cleaning

Prints solid black blocks so paper friction scrubs dust and glue residue off the thermal head. Run this when prints become faint, streaky, or uneven.

For stubborn residue: power off, open the cover, and gently wipe the thermal head with a cotton swab dipped in 90%+ isopropyl alcohol.

Alignment / Test Page

Prints a diagnostic page with alignment guides, grid lines, checkerboard, barcode, and QR code. Use it to check printhead dots, margins, and horizontal alignment.
